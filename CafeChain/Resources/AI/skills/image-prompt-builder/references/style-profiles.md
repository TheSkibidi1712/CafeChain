# Style profiles

- `clean-menu`: clean commercial cafe photography, neutral background, accurate product color.
- `vietnamese-cafe`: warm natural light, wood or stone surface, subtle local cafe mood.
- `fresh-fruit`: bright high-key light, fresh garnish, clear condensation, vibrant natural color.
- `dark-premium`: controlled low-key lighting, dark stone surface, premium highlights.
- `pastel-dessert`: soft diffused light, pastel background, playful but uncluttered styling.
- `minimal-studio`: seamless background, centered product, precise shadow and minimal props.

Chọn một profile chính. Không trộn `dark-premium` với `bright high-key` hoặc các chỉ dẫn ánh sáng mâu thuẫn.
